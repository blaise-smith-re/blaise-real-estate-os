# Automation Activation Plan

This is an engineering rollout map, not a second operating manual and not an authority grant. The
active Google Drive Business Operating Manual, Runtime Foundation Control Record, Runtime Registries
Sheet, and AI Execution Runbook control. When they differ, the live Drive controls win.

## Target operating model

The target is a deep operating-partner system, not a permanently read-only assistant:

| Operating surface | Target behavior | Relationship boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Daily Desk / Command Center | Continuously gather verified signals, reconcile dated commitments, rank the few opportunities that merit attention, and route exceptions | No activity-for-activity; best opportunities, not the biggest list |
| Lead Conversion & FUB Desk | Maintain the FUB system of record through one controlled service with exact targeting, idempotency, and independent read-back | Only this service may receive FUB write authority |
| Marketing & Relationship Engine | Discover and research opportunities; prepare scripts, drafts, talking points, and recommended approaches | Blaise owns consumer and referral-partner conversations and sends |
| Systems / Training / SOP Control | Detect drift and propose exact changes; later execute separately certified minor maintenance | Canonical Drive policy remains authoritative |
| Client Deliverables | Assemble source-grounded briefs, packets, and client-ready drafts | High-stakes/client-facing release remains governed by the applicable approval rule |
| Buyer, Seller, Market, and Transaction Operations | Orchestrate reachable systems and produce exact handoffs for Matrix/Northstar, Click Contracts, SkySlope, Ylopo, and ShowingTime | Browser-only action remains a Chrome-operator handoff until a certified lane exists |

ChatGPT remains the operating partner/orchestrator and cross-system QC layer. The execution packages
carry out bounded work. FUB owns identifiable prospects, relationship status, communication history,
tasks, promises, and dated next actions. Drive owns SOPs, templates, runbooks, and permanent assets.
Calendar owns appointments, showings, deadlines, and work blocks. No parallel CRM, task list,
calendar, or SOP library is created.

## Activation sequence

| Gate | What is built | What activates only after evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Runtime foundation | Event/report contract, exact registries, Operations Bus, zero-effect enforcement | Complete |
| 2 — Read adapters | Provider-neutral FUB read adapter and combined-pilot harness | Live FUB + Drive + Calendar pilot through `os.execution.v1` |
| 3 — Internal operating cadence | Daily Desk routing, Command Center ranking, client-prep flows, Decision Queue | Scheduled/unattended runs after scheduler certification |
| 4 — Internal record maintenance | Lead Conversion & FUB Desk, all 13 bounded write operations, exact targeting, effect budgets, idempotency/read-back | **Built and owner-authorized; restart + practical live smoke pending** |
| 5 — Event-driven orchestration | Department routing, exception management, cross-system reconciliation, browser handoff packets | Triggers after bounded smoke tests and observability/rollback controls |
| 6 — Continuous improvement | Findings capture, exact proposed patches, source drift detection | Bounded minor-maintenance writes only after their own certification |

## Standing boundaries

- AI prepares and maintains internal records autonomously; Blaise reviews immediately before SEND / SUBMIT / PUBLISH / SIGN / SPEND.
- Shared team infrastructure remains separately controlled; individual FUB record maintenance is owner-authorized.
- No live client data or credentials enter this repository.
- Incomplete retrieval, ambiguous identity, stale authority, or failed read-back returns HOLD.
- The build should be complete before activation; gates control effects, not intelligence depth.
