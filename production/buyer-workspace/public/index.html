Adding and tracking a new buyer
  1. In FUB, create the contact or open their existing record. Assign them to yourself and add the Buyer tag.
  2. Return here and choose Refresh buyers, then select their name. Contacts without a buyer type are listed separately.
  3. Review their brief, add showing feedback, and approve the exact note and any task before saving. Their records stay in FUB; client texts remain copy-only.

The list refreshes when you open this workspace or refresh it. It does not create contacts automatically from a showing. Buyer tags and Showing homes or Submitting offers identify buyers across stages; seller/renter-only and Trash records are excluded.
