# Digital content plan — {{address}}
Proposed event: {{date}} · {{time}}. Release state is private. Confirm event facts before publication.

## Prepared individual assets
Feed announcement; Story announcement; property-feature Story; engagement/poll Story; event reminder; day-of/Blaise-forward reminder; Reel cover; post-event relationship angle.
Mix property-first imagery with a different approved portrait for each Blaise-forward piece. The optional contact sheet is internal QA, never a publishable graphic.

## 20-second Reel shot plan
0–3s: exterior approach, stable frame, address/title overlay.
3–7s: kitchen/gathering feature; one verified benefit.
7–11s: living space and layout connection.
11–15s: strongest available outdoor or distinctive feature. Substitute an actual photographed feature when needed.
15–20s: exterior or Blaise on camera; one invitation to explore/ask a property question. Add confirmed day/time only when appropriate.
Use actual listing media or authorized on-site video. No simulated rooms, fake attendance or claim that a still-image plan is a finished Reel.

## Arrival/selfie prompt
After actual arrival: “I’m at {{address}}. One thing I’d make sure you notice is…” Finish with one verified feature and the confirmed invitation. Capture in a quiet location; do not include visitors without permission.

## At most two optional during-event Stories
1. One feature close-up explaining how the space works.
2. One useful property question answered accurately, without identifying a visitor.

## Post-event relationship angle
“Comparing homes in this area? I can help you organize the differences that matter to your search.”
Tie any event-specific reflection to actual observations. Do not invent traffic, feedback, urgency, offers or a successful event.

The day-of reminder is date-sensitive; do not publish “TODAY” before the actual date. A changed date/time requires fresh exports. No automated publishing or scheduling.
