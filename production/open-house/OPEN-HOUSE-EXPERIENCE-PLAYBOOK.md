# Open House Experience Playbook
Production method under the current Growth & Brand architecture. Not a competing Business SOP.

**VERIFY BACKSTAGE. SELL FRONTSTAGE.**

Open houses have four jobs: professionally support the property/listing; meet active buyers; build neighborhood recognition for Blaise; create future seller, referral and relationship opportunities. Property first, Blaise memorable. Lead generation is intentional, warm and useful.

## Ownership and current-only inputs
Resolve the active Canonical Source Map, then the exact current stable IDs for Growth & Brand Engine, Brand & Client Experience Blueprint, Lead Conversion & Relationship Management, Matrix/Northstar and AI Execution. `governance.json` is a locator cache, not independent authority. Never retrieve files marked LEGACY, RETIRED, SUPERSEDED or ARCHIVED for ordinary production. Record read time and content hash; refresh sources per new job. Historical transaction/listing evidence may still be relevant facts; it is not governance.

Growth owns business/channel strategy; Blueprint owns experience, voice and style; Lead SOP owns identifiable follow-up; Matrix runbook owns MLS mechanics; AI Execution owns boundaries. Codex operates browser retrieval, design, templates and engineering. Work/Blaise reviews business copy and release. This promotion does not edit Growth or claim a new canonical Source Map entry.

## 1. Source Pack
Start from address/MLS, listing agent and proposed date/time. Codex resolves the exact authenticated listing and criteria; reads current price, essential specs, HOA/CIC, taxes, district, strongest benefits, updates, useful remarks, access, relevant history and 3–5 active buyer alternatives. Record dates and source links privately. Use the listing's explicit HOA frequency; no HOA is not an unknown amount. Keep alternatives practical, with address, price, beds/baths/sqft, area/distance when observed, and one difference. They support conversion, not an automatic CMA.

Skim disclosures for no more than a few material host-level facts, normally 3–5 maximum or no major host-level issues identified. Do not perform forensic review, contract strategy or an inspection analysis. Unknowns, status history, conflicts and disclosure knowledge go into the private Host Brief/source register.

## 2. Media Pull
Use the authenticated Matrix browser's available viewer/download/export controls. Inspect live DOM only through the supported browser operator; no guessed API, credential/cookie extraction, direct MLS integration or unattended scraping. Preserve original site-delivered files and their provenance/hash before producing crops. Review the complete practical set, exclude duplicate/irrelevant/watermarked creative candidates, and classify exterior, kitchen, gathering, suite, outdoor, distinctive and secondary media. Codex assigns composition-fit/focal points; the engine selects by role and makes proportionate derivatives. Do not misrepresent property features.

Permission is separate: APPROVED / PERMITTED, NEEDS CONFIRMATION, DO NOT PUBLISH. Uncertain media rights allow internal draft preparation; they do not authorize public release. A missing source image prompts supported retrieval or an honest alternate actual view, never an invented room or abandoned placeholder.

## 3. Host Brief
Six short digital sections: property at a glance; five best talking points; do not freestyle; useful disclosed knowledge; 3–5 nearby alternatives; three natural conversation starters. Include taxes/district/HOA in useful at-a-glance facts. Keep source conflicts and limitations here. Access and operational instructions remain private. Phone-ready HTML, no extra print PDF.

## 4. Property Handout
Use the approved one-page Letter portrait master: address/price, conditional HOA, strongest two photos or appropriate real-image treatment, five fast facts, short confident positioning, benefit hierarchy, small selected portrait and clear Blaise contact block, Buy Sell Home Team/RE/MAX Results affiliation. Preserve the visual structure.

Show verified applicable HOA dues/frequency. Omit when no HOA unless strategically useful; resolve genuine unknowns backstage. Do not silently omit applicable ownership costs. Public copy should sell the strongest truthful benefits with Ryan Serhant conviction/energy and Aaron Kirman property expertise; support with human storytelling, clear explanations, hospitality and long-term relationship care. Never copy a celebrity voice.

Internal source/confidence labels, verification chatter, disclosure analysis, risk controls and do-not-freestyle text do not belong in marketing. Only an exact-asset legal, brokerage, MLS or truthfulness requirement permits sourced minimal public disclosure. No invented legal boilerplate. A word-filter is a guard, not legal compliance or a substitute for editorial review.

## 5. Sign-In
One Letter portrait page, about nine generous rows: Name, Phone, Email, Working with an agent? Y/N. No survey/discovery prompt, no portrait. Name, phone and affiliations supply light branding. Completed sheets are restricted contact records. The custom print stack is exactly these two pages; the evergreen Buyer Guide is optional and separate.

The explicit owner brief retains the agent-status field/hosting step for this system. The current Lead SOP says not to proactively qualify outside-agent status. Record this narrow conflict for Work/Blaise reconciliation; do not propagate the open-house instruction to other lead routes or modify the SOP.

## 6. Social Kit
Prepare individual feed and Story announcements, feature Story, engagement/poll Story, event reminder, day-of reminder, Reel cover with a 15–25 second shot plan, arrival/selfie prompt, at most two optional during-event ideas, and a post-event relationship angle. Property-first and Blaise-forward pieces serve different jobs. Rotate approved portraits by role, pose, framing, background and recent use. Sign-in/internal documents remain portrait-free. A social board is optional internal QA only.

Derive weekday and time from the case. TODAY requires the actual event date; recorded arrival and post-event outcomes require actual evidence. Proposed event-copy previews remain drafts privately. Never fabricate visitors, interest, sends or success. Rendering is not publication.

## 7. Five-Sign Routing
Codex reads the actual address in a map/browser, identifies major approaches and true turns, and selects exactly five nonredundant slots. Return Sign 1–5, intersection/location, arrow and approaching direction, reason, efficient placement order, map/date provenance and on-site checks. Verify route connectivity and avoid unnecessary stops. Maps cannot confirm legal placement, sightlines, construction or safe stopping; Blaise judges those on site. No static route is inherited from Lever Street.

## 8. Day-of Setup
Confirm instructions; arrive with time to walk the home and become ready before the first visitor. Address presentation, authorized lights/blinds/temp/odor, handouts, private sign-in station, optional guides/cards, five signs and one quick arrival recording. Do not modify the home beyond authority or show access codes/visitors on camera. Keep Host Brief on phone.

## 9. Hosting
WELCOME → GIVE SPACE → DISCOVERY → VALUE → REPRESENTATION STATUS → USEFUL NEXT STEP.
Speak naturally: “What brought you out today—this house specifically or are you looking around here?” “What are you trying to find that you haven’t found yet?” When useful: “If this one isn’t quite it, I’ve got a couple nearby I’d probably have you look at too.” Use alternatives to demonstrate useful preparation. Respect known obligations and visitor pace; earn a next step without turning the visit into an intake interview.

## 10. Close-Down
Allow 10–15 minutes at the home plus driving time: secure sign-in, capture meaningful private context, collect materials, restore presentation, check doors/lights/access, retrieve and count all five signs. Do not claim lockup or retrieval before checking.

## 11. Attendee/FUB Handoff
Reconcile meaningful visitors as represented buyer, active unrepresented buyer, future buyer/nurture, neighbor/potential seller/referral, or casual/no actionable relationship. Read the exact existing FUB record and recent activity before an authorized write. Keep useful property source/contact/context/actual criteria and one worthwhile next action. No giant CRM record, automatic task, inferred consent or automatic stage/tag change. Current Lead/FUB rules and exact write authority control; this generator makes no FUB changes.

## 12. Listing-Agent Recap
After the real event, draft a short recap: attendance count/method, meaningful reactions, second-look signals, relevant questions, operational issues. Do not invent outcomes or share contact details unnecessarily. Blaise reviews; no automatic send.

## 13. Drive Filing
Repository owns production source and tests. Drive owns durable business artifacts and property/event records. Resolve the existing correct folder; keep source pack/media, approved handout/sign-in, completed restricted sign-in, social actually used, useful publication proof, recap and meaningful notes. Read back any authorized filing by file ID/location. Local working folders are not proof of durable filing. The prepared promotion package is not filed or canonically indexed until Work performs business QC and filing.

## 14. Learning Loop
Capture only reusable lessons supported by evidence: sign performance, visitor quality, useful openers, repeated questions, response-driving social, capture friction, buyer/seller/referral outcomes. Improve templates when evidence supports it. No report for ordinary event noise.

## Finish / release
Validate identity/price/HOA, media and governance provenance, public/private separation, contact consistency, two single-page PDFs, dimensions and visual fit. Keep draft readiness separate from hosting/media authorization. Work/Blaise reviews exact assets; only a separately authorized action releases, sends or schedules. No automatic MLS/FUB/Calendar mutations, external events or spend.
