material source-supported deltas to the capable orchestrator for that lane. Apply the current
Runbook for exact ownership, an existing task's own assignment, semantic deduplication, note-only
closeout, execute versus preview, partial success and independent readback. No required note/task
for unchanged information. Native logs are not duplicated. Logging does not call or send.
Appointment invitations remain off without exact outward authority.

Shared configuration needs the authorized affected-scope owner. Do not require separate Brent
approval for Blaise-scoped work already authorized by Blaise under the Runbook. These wrappers
have no shared-configuration write tool. Do not change ownership or another agent's tasks.

Use supported task pagination and `_completeness` before claiming full retrieval:
`returned_count`, `total_count`, `has_more`, `capped`; supported `fetch_all`,
`due_on`/`due_from`/`due_to` and `due_timezone=America/Chicago`.
Legacy `due` is not completeness-safe. Name search may omit Trash; zero/one match is not global
nonexistence/uniqueness proof.
