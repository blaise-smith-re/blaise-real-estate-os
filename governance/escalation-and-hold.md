# Escalation and capability boundaries

Current authority: live Drive Manual and AI Runbook through `source-registry.json`.
Hold only the affected action for unresolved identity, missing controlling evidence, a genuine
business/legal decision, human authentication or an unauthorized consequential effect. Name the
exact target, gap, next owner and resume condition; continue independent useful work.

The local runtime is manual-only with no persistent host/live adapters. This is not a global
scheduling HOLD. Hosted ChatGPT tasks have their own actual prompts, enabled states and results.
Documentation does not activate one. Compatibility wrappers retain their actual grants/denials.
Read-only workers route material client deltas to the capable orchestrator/FUB maintenance lane.
A handoff is not execution; verify its receipt. No grants or credentials are expanded here.

Calendar is optional except for a dependent recorded-event claim, and advisory by default.
Mutation needs an explicit exact-action request. FUB appointments are not Calendar mutations.
Work owns accepted durable guidance; Codex may perform expressly delegated canonical changes.
Ordinary authorized in-place correction is not canonical-file replacement or a new approval vote.

Preserve external-send, submission/publication, signature, spending, shared-team, vendor, credential
and destructive boundaries. Check real supported browser/connector capability; do not require a
Claude-only hop or infer inherited sessions. Preserve current Ylopo restrictions and client-link rules.

For a failed update preserve source, target, proposed delta, last verified state, owner and resume
condition in existing restricted case/maintenance context when accessible. If no persistence lane
exists, say the checkpoint is conversation-only. No fake monitoring or promised background retry.
