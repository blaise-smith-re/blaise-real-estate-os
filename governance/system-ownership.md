## Boundaries that bind this repository

- **A lead is not managed in Google Drive, ChatGPT, or Claude.** Every viable lead and active client
  must have one clear dated next action **in Follow Up Boss**.
- **Google Drive does not manage lead stages or follow-up tasks.** Every permanent asset has one
  canonical owner and location. Improve the current asset; never create a competing version.
- **A deadline is not controlled until it is on the Calendar** with the correct date, time,
  description, and owner. FUB, Click, or a transaction summary may *identify* one.
- **Ylopo and other IDX values remain reported data** until material facts are verified against the
  current MLS record.
- **This repository owns none of the above.** It owns agent definitions, skills, tests, source
  pointers, tool policy, and engineering documentation.
